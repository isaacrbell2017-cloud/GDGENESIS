# GdGenesis

<cy>AI decoration generator</c> for the level editor, by <cg>Litchin</c>.

## How to use
1. Select the block(s) you want decorated, or pick <cy>Whole Level</c> to decorate your entire layout.
2. Press the editor's pause button and tap the <cg>GdGenesis</c> sparkle button (or your activation key).
3. Pick a quality: <cy>Featured</c>, <co>Epic</c>, <cp>Legendary</c> or <cr>Mythic</c>.
4. Type a prompt like <cy>neon glow</c>, <cy>space with dots fill</c> or <cy>dungeon chains</c> and press <cg>Generate</c>.

Don't like the result? Press the editor's Undo and generate again.

## Features
- **Quality tiers**: higher tiers add edge glow, glow orbs, fill, sparkles, background colors and color sections.
- **Fill**: deco inside the blocks: Glow, Rim, Dots, Tiles, Checker or Solid (fills enclosed holes). Fill pieces are always No Touch.
- **Whole Level**: decorates the entire layout with colors that shift section by section.
- **Modules**: force Lights, Edge Glow, Fill, Aura, Particles, Chains, Grass, Bushes, Clouds, Spires or BG Colors on or off, and set the density.

## Modes
- **Offline** - instant, no internet needed.
- **Claude API** - add your API key in the mod settings and Claude designs the deco from your prompt.
- **Auto** (default) - Claude when a key is set, offline otherwise.
