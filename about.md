# GdGenesis

<cy>AI decoration generator</c> for the level editor, by <cg>Litchin</c>.

## How to use
1. Select the block(s) you want decorated. A whole layout works too.
2. Press the editor's pause button and tap the <cg>GdGenesis</c> sparkle button.
3. Type a prompt like <cy>neon glow</c>, <cy>space with stars</c> or <cy>dungeon chains</c> and press <cg>Generate</c>.

Don't like the result? Press the editor's Undo and generate again.

## Modes
- **Offline** - instant, no internet needed.
- **Claude API** - add your API key in the mod settings and Claude designs the deco from your prompt.
- **Auto** (default) - Claude when a key is set, offline otherwise.

## Key Activation
Geode > GdGenesis > gear > turn on **Key Activation**, pick your key, then apply. The key opens GdGenesis straight from the editor.
